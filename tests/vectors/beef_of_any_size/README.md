# The rows of a BEEF of any size, as fixtures for the incremental hashers

Source: the corpus `corpus/beef-of-any-size` of bsv-stack-lean at `f061506`
(`rows.json` and `bytes/`, generated by its `tools/beef_rows_export.py` from the
Lean scenario rows of `lean/BeefOfAnySize/Scenarios.lean`). The bytes are copied
unchanged; each file keeps its row's name.

- `<row>.bin`: the 47 rows at or under 64 KiB, the rows the corpus commits.
- `rows.txt`: one line per row of the corpus, all 52: the name, the length,
  `fixture` or `generated`, the SHA-256 of the bytes as `rows.json` pins it, and
  the double SHA-256 computed with Python's `hashlib` when the fixtures were
  copied. The five `generated` rows (the deep chains and the wide BUMP of 16,384
  leaves, 0.59 MB to 6.1 MB) are not copied; `tests/hash_stream.rs` reads them
  from the directory named by `BSV_RS_BEEF_ROWS_DIR` when it is set (the corpus's
  `bytes/` after running its tool) and names them as skipped otherwise.

`tests/hash_stream.rs` holds `sha256`, `sha256d`, `Sha256` and `Sha256d` to
these digests for every chunking it tries. The bytes are BEEF; no test here
parses them.
