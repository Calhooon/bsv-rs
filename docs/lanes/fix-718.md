# #718: the identity and contacts canonical PushDrop checks against the reference

Base `dd78a01d` (`origin/beta`'s tip at the lane's start); rebased onto
`ba59e71d`, PR #717's landing (the #616 fix to `canonical_refusal`), which
merged while the lane worked. None of the four sites calls `canonical_refusal`,
so #717's change reaches none of them; the rebase conflicted only in the
records (`CHANGELOG.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `src/script/CLAUDE.md`), resolved as
beta's text with this lane's edits. The issue, from #717's rule read: that
read confirmed the shared canonical refusal against `PushDropValidation.ts:54-79`
at `ts-stack@edf6e03` for the five `decodeCanonicalPushDrop` sites, but did not
read the identity and contacts canonical-script checks, which might hold the
old envelope-before-bounds order or their own words.

## The difference and its fix

`authenticate_contact` now reads the token as `PushDrop.decode` reads it at
its default position (`decode` in `contacts_auth.rs`): no first chunk
`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'data')`, a first chunk without
data `script.chunks[0].data must have value`, a key `PublicKey::from_bytes`
refuses in `Point.fromDER`'s words (`point_message`, the shared helper of
`pushdrop_position.rs`, unchanged by #717's diff), then
`PushDrop::decode_reference_before` (#618's reading, `pub(crate)` now): the
second chunk never read, the fields by `reference_fields`. Every refusal is
`Error::IdentityError` with the reference's words, so the count, the bounds
and the envelope that follow refuse in the order and words the reference's
do. A key the reference reads and `from_bytes` refuses (a compressed x at or
above p, #498's row) keeps the native words, a stated residue. The two
identity sites are unchanged: their refusals are skips on both sides.

## The classifiers

The old words are the shared decode's and stay at its other callers
(`Script too short for PushDrop`, `Expected OP_CHECKSIG after pubkey`:
`pushdrop.rs`, `pushdrop_position.rs`, the 489 and 618 tests;
`unknown point format`: the primitives and the 550 test). No test, vector or
conformance class matched them at the contacts site: every caller drops the
output or replaces the words. The contacts words in `tests/` (the 537, 542 and
544 files and vectors) are the reference's after-read words, unchanged; their
files pass.

## Residues

- A compressed key with x at or above p: the reference reduces it and reads
  on; `from_bytes` refuses it here in native words (#498's row, #183).
- The update and removal refusals: the reference's carries the inner words as
  `cause`; this crate's error has no cause chain (older than #718; the words
  are now the reference's should one be added).
