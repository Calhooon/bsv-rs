## The issue's comment

#845's comment records a decision, not a wait: the writer half is a typed id (the tie-break of 2026-11-10, three reads, T1 three of three; the owner's word on #843; `docs/design/843-identity-and-framing.md`, section 3). `pub struct Txid([u8; 32])` in wire order; text only through `from_hex` and `FromStr` (64 hex digits, either case, no trim), shown as lower-case hex; byte doors that name their order, no `From<[u8; 32]>`; `TransactionInput::source_txid: Option<Txid>`, the input's fields public; `TransactionInput::new(Txid, u32)`; `Transaction::txid()` beside `id()`. This lane builds that. Two parts of the same record are other pull requests of #843 and are not built here: the `Beef` doors that take a txid ("in the same cut as the private `txs`", which has not landed: `Beef::txs` and `bumps` are public at the base) and the wallet's display-order `TxId` alias (W1, "built in #843's third PR" with the one id type; 58 library sites by the callers read). The reader half (`from_json`) landed with #844.
