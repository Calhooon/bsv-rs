## Residue

`merge_raw_tx` after two BUMPs holding its txid: this crate's
`try_to_validate_bump_index` takes the earliest BUMP holding it as a txid leaf,
the reference's `#tryToValidateBumpIndex` (`:1329-1340`) its index's, the last
(probed under Node 22: `bumpIndex 1`). The index byte written differs for that
merge order; no verdict and no path a reader attaches depends on it, but
both atomic writers carry the BUMP the entry's index names (`atomic_closure`;
the reference's `#copySelectedTransactions`, `:448-470`), so
`to_binary_atomic` of that entry carries the earliest BUMP where
`toBinaryAtomic` carries the last, and its reread attaches another path (the
rule read of PR #758). Filed as #757.

The reference's `#addInputProof` also overwrites a `merklePath` a transaction
already carries (`:658-660`), where this crate keeps a caller's own; not read
further here.
