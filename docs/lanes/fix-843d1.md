One shape stays ours and is stated: the reference lends a mutable array and finds an in-place edit at its next serialization; here the slice is shared and an edit goes through the write methods or `from_chunks`.

`Cache::write` lends the chunks to a closure (`write(|chunks| ..)`) where the brief named `write(&mut self) -> &mut Vec<ScriptChunk>`: `OnceLock::get_mut` answers an `Option`, `get_mut_or_init` is unstable on 1.98.1 (E0658, `once_cell_get_mut`), and the crate denies `unwrap`, `expect` and `panic`; the closure keeps the door total with no panic path. The chunks stay in the lock during the edit.
