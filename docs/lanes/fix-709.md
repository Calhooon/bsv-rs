## The rules, each read from the YAML

1. **Every workflow read for a dispatch or a call.** A job's code (comments
   dropped, a `\` continuation joined) dispatches a workflow when
   `gh workflow run <file>` or `gh api ... /dispatches` stands at a command
   position (the line's start after `- ` and `run:`, or after `&&`, `||`,
   `;`, `|`, `$(`, `then`, `do`; so an `echo`'s text and a comment are not
   read: `bench.yml:152`, `sync-main-into-beta.yml:166`), and calls one with a
   `uses:` naming `.github/workflows/<file>`. In `nightly.yml` such a job is a
   heavy job, with a `needs:` path to every other heavy job. Outside it, the
   job is refused unless its workflow starts by a hand act alone (the `on:`
   keys `workflow_dispatch` and a `push` of `tags:` with no `branches` or
   `paths`) or the record `STATED` names it with its reason.
   - `release.yml` (`on: push: tags: ['v*']`): its six calls (`ci.yml`,
     `docs.yml`, `msrv.yml`, `features.yml`, `machete.yml`, `bench.yml`) pass
     by the trigger. A version tag is the owner's release ritual
     (`RELEASING.md`), cut by hand, with the nightly's dispatch a step before
     a minor tag; the two are sequenced by the person cutting it, not by
     `needs:`.
   - `sync-main-into-beta.yml` (`on: push: branches: [main]` and
     `workflow_dispatch`): its dispatch of `ci.yml` on the sync branch
     (`:219-222`, under `steps.pick.outputs.push == 'true'`: only when the
     sync pushed picks of main's commits, on a push to main or a hand rerun)
     is the sync PR's CI (a `GITHUB_TOKEN` push starts no run). The run is
     on the sync branch, built from beta, so it runs beta's `ci.yml`: both
     matrices capped at six, at most nine jobs at once, not main's 22 (the
     workflow read's correction). It has fired zero times on record: seven
     sync runs since 2026-10-25 (four in the week to 2026-11-03), every one
     "nothing to sync", and no `workflow_dispatch` run of `ci.yml` on
     `sync/main-into-beta`. A push to main is no hand act the rule can read,
     so it is named in `STATED`; the run is **not** sequenced with the
     nightly (beside a complete run's six, 15 of the twenty): a stated
     residual for the keeper, to record as now or to sequence by a poll for
     a running nightly in the sync's "Start the PR's CI" step (the issue's
     "today: none" did not count it). Main's own push run of `ci.yml`, which
     the same push starts and no rule reads, is the more frequent overlap. The keeper's word (2026-11-03 19:1xZ): the sync's dispatch is recorded as the
     stated residual now (never fired on record; at most nine jobs; beta's `ci.yml` on
     the sync branch); the poll in the sync's start step is row #714, held until the
     dispatch ever fires during a complete run; a shared `concurrency` group is refused
     (one pending run per group, so a queued sync and a queued nightly dispatch would
     cancel each other).
   - A `STATED` entry the workflows no longer carry is refused, so the record
     cannot outlive its site.
2. **`!cancelled()` after the shards.** Every heavy job of the nightly that
   reaches `mutants` through `needs:` carries `!cancelled()` in its `if:` and
   no `always()`. Judged today: `ci-full-beta`, `ci-full-main`, `bench`,
   `fuzz`. `mutation-score` keeps its `always()`: one runner, no dispatch, not
   heavy.
3. **The give-up bound.** Every job with `gh run watch` (in any workflow)
   reads its `timeout-minutes`, the budget's minutes
   (`budget="$((N * 60 - SECONDS))"`, read whole since #713's fold), the
   poll count (`seq 1 P`) and its interval (`sleep I`, the largest of each);
   the larger of N and ceil(P × I / 60) is at most
   `timeout-minutes` minus five. Today 55 and 5 under 60 for both waits. A
   wait missing any of the four figures is refused naming what it read.
4. **The wait's `--workflow`, both directions (#713's fold).** The set of
   workflows a waiting job dispatches equals the set its `gh run` lines list
   (`--workflow X`, `--workflow=X`, `-w X`); none listed is refused. A
   dispatch or a list naming a variable (`gh workflow run "$WF"`) is read
   through the job's one literal assignment of it (a shell `WF=bench.yml` at
   a command's start, or a step's `env:` key `WF: bench.yml`); unassigned,
   assigned two values, or assigned an expression (`$`, `${{ }}`), it is
   refused as unreadable.
5. **A watch for every dispatch job before another (#713's fold).** A job
   of the nightly that dispatches a workflow and has a job after it (by
   `needs:`, directly or through another) carries a `gh run watch` per
   dispatch; a reusable call is its own wait (the caller ends with the
   called run). Judged today: `ci-full-beta`, `bench`; `ci-full-main` is the
   last and waits for nothing.
6. **The watch bounded by the budget (#713's fold).** Every `gh run watch`
   runs under `timeout "$budget"` (`timeout "${budget}"` and
   `timeout $budget` read the same): `ci-full-beta`'s block reads the
   watch's exit 124 as the give-up, which is `timeout`'s status, so a
   `timeout` with options, a literal or another variable is refused. The
   budget is the one assignment `budget="$((N * 60 - SECONDS))"` (the floor
   `budget=1` aside, which only lowers it), and rules 3 and 6 read the same
   figures from one reader: rule 3 takes N from that assignment alone, the
   largest poll count and the largest interval (a unit suffix such as
   `sleep 2m` is unreadable and refused, a smaller decoy before the loop no
   reading), so a budget doubled inside `$((...))` is refused by both.

## Limits

String-level reading, as the older guard's: a dispatch built from a variable
the job does not assign one literal is read as a dispatch of the text `$WF`,
heavy but named by no file (refused in a wait since #713's fold); the strength
read's other evasions stay open and stated: a dispatch behind an env prefix,
`if`, `timeout` or a group (no command position), `curl`, `github-script` or a
marketplace dispatch action, `!cancelled() || true` and a step-level
`always()`, a second `workflow_dispatch`-only dispatcher workflow, a
four-space `jobs:` indentation, and the `STATED` record keyed by its triple
alone (a changed trigger stays fresh); a dispatch inside a script file the workflow runs
(`.github/scripts/*`) is not read; a `uses:` of another repository's workflow
is read by its file name. The scoped check of `354cabd9` named a probe no guard reads:
`(( budget *= 2 ))` after the one `budget=` assignment doubles the watch's
bound while rules 3 and 6 read the assignment alone (a stated residue, no
rule in the second fold).
