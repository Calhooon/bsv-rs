## A finding for the keeper

The brief's witness 1 (a scripted facilitator's 4,097-output answer refused as
the resource limit, the reputation unchanged) is not what the reference does
at its defaults: `completeTrackedLookup` runs `normalizeLookupAnswer` before
`assertTrackedLookupAnswer` (`:2385-2392`), and the normalizer holds the
constant `MAX_LOOKUP_OUTPUTS` (`:254`, `:395`), so a custom facilitator's
4,097-output answer is `Malformed lookup response` with `recordFailure`
there; only the HTTPS facilitator's own count (`:859-860`) is the neutral
limit. The same constant makes a `maxOutputs` raised past 4096 admit nothing.
This lane followed the brief (one configured bound, neutral on every path)
and states the difference in `CLAUDE.md` and the changelog; the alternative,
the reference's two-step reading, is a three-line change in
`LookupRequestOptions::assert_tracked_answer` if the keeper rules for it.
