compares `to_beef` with a model of 0.3.34's, which merged clones carrying

## A held txid replaced at the end (the cold read; closed by the fold)

The reference replaces a held entry in place (`#replaceOrAppendTx`,
`:531-540`); this crate removed it and appended the new one, at
`merge_transaction` and `merge_raw_tx` alike, before #764 and at `8db1878a`. Where no
dependency orders the two, the sort keeps insertion order (proven entries in

bytes differ: two unrelated transactions A and B, each merged raw (both proven

(`mergeTransaction` with its path, `mergeTransaction` without, or `mergeRawTx`):
the reference writes A then B, this crate B then A, on `d14f560d` and on the
tip (measured with the edf6e03 bundle under Node v22.18.0 and a scratch test
on both trees). Older than #764 and outside its nine cases. The fold replaces
in place through one helper (`replace_or_append`) at every door that replaces
