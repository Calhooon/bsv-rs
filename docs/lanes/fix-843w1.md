## The decision

W1, 3 of 3 (`docs/design/843-identity-and-framing.md`, section 9 item 1; the owner's word of 2026-11-10 on #843). Built as decided, with the captain's six rulings of 2026-11-15: (1) `Txid` gets no serde, the three field-level adapters are retyped over it; (2) `Utxo::txid` is not this lane's; (3) an input with neither a txid nor a source stays the stated cell; (4) the class machine reads the alias, the byte doors under `src/wallet/` and `src/auth/`, and the `[u8; 32]` holders outside `src/transaction/`; (5) the result BEEF's two hand reversals become `Txid::from_wire_bytes` at the site, that file the one entry of the architecture test's list; (6) `hex_txid` and `hex_txid_vec_option` are retyped in place with the base's visibility.

Nothing was added to `Txid`: the compile asked for no impl (no holder asks `Default`, `PartialOrd` or `Ord` of the id; every serde of a holder is a field-level adapter). Nothing under `src/transaction/` changed (`git diff 471bdaf6 --stat -- src/transaction` is empty), and no BEEF door's signature.
