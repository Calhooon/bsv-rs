```
  --> src/auth/transports/socketio/app_event.rs:36:20
```

The tip passes the same build on the same lockfile, and on 0.3.34.

**The form (the captain's ruling: the manifest's floor stays).** `try_recv` is one poll of the stream's `next`: `futures::FutureExt::now_or_never(futures::StreamExt::next(..))`. Its answers against the method's (`try_recv().ok()`), read in futures-channel 0.3.34's source (`next_message`, `poll_next`) and run:

| Queue | `try_recv().ok()` on 0.3.32 and later | one poll of `next` | Same |
|---|---|---|---|
| an item ready | `Ok(item)`, `Some(item)` | `Ready(Some(item))`, `Some(item)` | yes |
| empty, open | `Err(Empty)`, `None` | `Pending`, `None` | yes |
| closed, drained | `Err(Closed)`, `None` | `Ready(None)`, `None` | yes |

One thing the poll does that the method did not: it registers a waker (a no-op one) and looks at the queue once more, as `poll_next` does against a sender racing the registration; an item found by that second look is an item ready. The next `recv` replaces the waker with its own (`a_recv_after_an_empty_try_recv_is_woken_by_the_next_event_843d23`, a counting waker).

**What the run at the floor found.** The same form and the three answers in a crate of their own (kept in the lane's out folder, `floor-crate/`), the whole family held at one version: on 0.3.11 (the lowest `futures` 0.3 crates.io has not yanked), 0.3.12 and 0.3.13 the closed answer panicked, `Receiver::next_message called after None`: those futures-channel versions panic on a poll after the stream's end, 0.3.14 and later answer `None`. The PR's `recv` had the same exposure (a second `recv` after `None`). So the receiver holds its channel in an `Option` and drops it when the channel answers its end; no call polls after that, and `None` then `None` again is the receiver's own answer. With that: 3 of 3 on 0.3.11, 0.3.12, 0.3.13, 0.3.14, 0.3.30, 0.3.31, 0.3.32 and 0.3.34.

**In tree.** Four unit tests beside the type (an item ready in the order sent; an empty open queue, twice, the queue still open after; a closed queue after its queued items, asked again by `recv` and `try_recv`; the wake after an empty `try_recv`) and `tests/socketio_app_event_receiver_843d23.rs` (2), green with the family at 0.3.31 (a scratch copy of the tip on the RED build's lockfile) and at 0.3.34 (the worktree's). The tree's graph does not resolve with futures-util below 0.3.31 (every futures-util from 0.3.0 to 0.3.30 is yanked on crates.io, read from its index on 2026-11-12), so the versions below it are the standalone crate's alone, and the drop at the end has no in-tree catcher: with it removed the four unit tests and the integration file stay green on 0.3.31 and 0.3.34 (the cold read of the fold, a plant); on the family held at 0.3.13 by a hand-written lockfile the same removal panics, `Receiver::next_message called after None`.
