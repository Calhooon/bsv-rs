The issue's correction: it reads a re-serialization at `merge_raw_tx`. On beta
the parse keeps the bytes (`Transaction::from_binary` sets `raw_bytes_cache`,
`transaction.rs:180-187`) and `to_binary` returns a clone of them
(`:1373-1381`), so the held bytes are hashed; the waste was one `sha256d` and
one clone of the body per merged raw entry, while the source entry holds its
txid already.

## Why the source's txid is the hash

`BeefTx::with_txid` is `pub(crate)`. Its callers in `src/` at `c96f3c7e`:

1. `src/transaction/beef.rs:386` `push_transaction`: the txid its caller computed from the transaction it passes (`t.id()` in `merge_beef`; the collected ancestors' ids in `to_beef`).
2. `src/transaction/beef.rs:396` `merge_raw_tx`: the hash of the bytes it was given (now through `store_raw_entry`, `merge_entry.rs`).
3. `src/transaction/beef/collect.rs:173`: the decoder's hash of the bytes it read.
4. `src/transaction/beef/merge_graph.rs:57`: `tx.id()` of the transaction stored.

On the tip a fifth site, `merge_entry.rs`'s `store_raw_entry`, is the body
callers 2 and the new door share. A source entry on `merge_beef`'s raw branch
holds bytes; its `txid` is one of those hashes of them, or `None`, and then
`txid()` hashes them (or the parse of them). An entry mutated through `tx_mut`
dropped its bytes and txid (`beef_tx.rs:211-219`) and takes the parsed branch,
`push_transaction(t.id(), ..)`, unchanged. So in every reachable state the
txid passed is the one `merge_raw_tx` would compute.
