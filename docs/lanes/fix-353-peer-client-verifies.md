## The finding (issue #353, from the toolbox's issuance design read, verified at beta's tip and `ts-stack@edf6e03`)

`Peer::start`'s client callback (`src/auth/peer.rs:226-284`) marks a session authenticated on an `initialResponse` with no check and routes a general message to the callbacks (`:298-307`) with no session or signature check; `handle_incoming_message` (`:605-625`, `process_general_message` `:986-1013`) verifies and is installed by `start_server` only (`:349-355`); `initiate_handshake` drops its pinned identity (`:704`); `process_initial_response_inner` continues on a failed nonce (`:857-874`); `process_general_message` verifies no `yourNonce` and requires no nonce. The reference's `Peer.ts:886-930` and `:1270-1314` verify every one of these and throw in the words the issue quotes. Security-relevant: a client on `start()` takes its server's answers unproven; the toolbox's storage client runs that way.
