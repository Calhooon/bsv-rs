## Where the check lives

In the parsers' door, `src/identity/evidence.rs`, not in
`Transaction::from_beef`. `from_beef` does the target pick and the linking
walk (`Beef::find_atomic_transaction`, `add_input_proof`) and nothing of the
check: no bound, no spent-outpoint set, no refusal of an unproven input
without its source. It is a public door every module reads, so the check
there would change every caller. The port reads the BEEF directly
(`Beef::from_binary`, then `find_txid` for the target and every input's
source, `find_bump` for a proof) rather than the `source_transaction` links:
`add_input_proof` links a repeated unproven parent in full once and hands
later inputs a stub with its inputs unlinked, and the reference's walk pops
in stack order (the last input first), so a walk over the links could reach
the stub before the full copy and refuse a complete BEEF.

The reference's caller does not skip every refusal: `IdentityEvidenceVerifier.parse`
(`identityUtils.ts:630-634` at `edf6e03`) rethrows `limit` (and `timeout`),
`parseResults` lets it out, and the wallet's discovery rethrows it
(`Wallet.ts:950-955`). The recorder confirms it: every over-limit shape's
`parseResults` outcome is `refused: Transaction evidence: limit`, and #553's
vector already recorded the same verdict for its two 256 KiB tokens. So
`admit` skips `invalid-evidence` and answers `limit` as
`Error::IdentityError("Transaction evidence: limit")`, the reference's words
through the existing variant; no new error variant. This departs from the
brief's "skip on any refusal" by the reference's code; the keeper may rule
otherwise.

`TxidNotInBump` is raised by `Beef::verify_structure` and by the streaming
reader's `check_bump_index` (`src/transaction/beef_stream.rs:2068-2077`); the
#728 lane record (`docs/lanes/fix-728.md:68`) and its CHANGELOG entry name
`verify_structure` alone. Neither is this door's check: `parseEvidence` asks
no BUMP index of its leaf.
