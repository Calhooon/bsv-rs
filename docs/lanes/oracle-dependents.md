The CLI's `oracle-dependents` brief (`git -C ~/bsv/bsv-wallet-cli show origin/beta:docs/lanes/oracle-dependents.md`).

**The work.**
1. `docs/DEPENDENTS.md`: one row per consumer: crate, its pin today, the features it takes, native or wasm32 (and the slice, matching #80's list), the version it runs in production where known (`[D]` where told), its captain (the CLI's and the toolbox's are known; the workers' are the keeper's until named), the re-pin plan with a date for the three behind (`=0.4.5` storage, `0.6.0` agent-template, `=0.7.2` overlay) and for the apps.
2. The CI job `dependents` in `ci.yml`: for each consumer whose pin admits the PR's version (today the 0.8 rows: the CLI, the toolbox, bsv-tracker, wallet-infra, chaintracks, messagebox, middleware-rs, middleware-cloudflare), check out its default branch at a pinned commit (recorded in `docs/dependents.lock`, a small TOML the pin-move PRs update), apply `[patch.crates-io] bsv-rs = { path = ... }` to the PR's tree, and `cargo check` it with the features and target its row names (wasm32 for the workers), `cargo test` where its tests are hermetic (say per row which). A row that does not build fails the job and names the consumer. Private repositories need a read token: name the secret the job expects and stop there; the captain asks the owner.
3. The CI job `semver` in `ci.yml`: `cargo-semver-checks check-release --baseline-rev <last tag on the line>` on every PR; a breaking change fails on a PR whose version bump is a patch or a minor under 1.0 as the tool reads it; the report shows the tool's output on `beta` today.
4. `RELEASING.md`: a paragraph on the canary order (the toolbox's and the CLI's beta lines take a bsv-rs beta prerelease; the workers and LOW re-pin by their captains after the CLI's gate), pointing at `docs/DEPENDENTS.md`; #100 writes the full policy.

**Bounds.** Read the dependents, never write to them (a re-pin is their captain's PR). No edit under `src/`, `Cargo.toml` untouched. No secret, no hostname of a deployment, no account id in anything you write.

**Report.** `REPORT: STATUS done|blocked`, the base and tip commits, the table, the per-consumer `cargo check` tails against your tree, the semver-checks tail, the secret the job needs if any, the six tests each choice passes.
