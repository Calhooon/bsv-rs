## The gate, and why this shape

The test compiles the generator's own source as a module (`#[path]`) and calls `generate`
with a temporary directory. The alternative, a CI step that builds the generator's crate and
diffs two directories, costs a second build of the crate with `full,http` in another
workspace (53 s here in release with four jobs, cold; no cache key carries it) and leaves the
comparison in shell. As a test binary the gate reuses the build `fixtures_replay` already made
in the same step, runs in the whole suite on every platform of the matrix (`fuzz/corpus/** -text`
in `.gitattributes` keeps the bytes on a CRLF checkout), and clippy and the mutation selection
read it. The generator's `main` (its argument reading) is the one part no CI build compiles.

Where it runs: the Test Suite's whole run (`--test '*'`), and by name in the `fixtures` gate of
`checks`, beside `fixtures_replay`, the repository's other regenerate-and-compare check. The
name is needed there: a pull request's Test Suite runs the binaries its change reaches by
module, and a change to `fuzz/corpus/`, to the generator or to a vector names none.

What it costs: the run is 3.0 to 4.2 s (debug, macOS arm64, six runs); the build beside
`fixtures_replay` is one test binary's compile and link. The Linux figure is CI's, not measured
here.

| Test | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `the_checked_in_corpus_is_what_corpus_gen_writes_byte_for_byte_802` | every written seed checked in with its bytes; every other checked-in seed of a `KEPT` family with its count |
| `the_generator_runs_past_a_recorded_table_among_the_wire_message_files_802` | the finding: the run reaches its last target with tables in the wire directory, and a table seeds nothing |
| `the_seed_counts_take_the_empty_seeds_in_and_name_them_802` | `COUNTS` (seeds per target, of them empty), `EMPTY` by name, five of them the generator's, every corpus a target of `fuzz/Cargo.toml` |
| `a_seed_edited_missing_or_unlisted_is_refused_naming_it_802` | the comparison on seven planted trees, each refusal by its exact message |
| `the_fixtures_gate_of_checks_runs_this_binary_802` | the `ci.yml` line, and five plants of it refused (removed, commented, `\|\| true`, `--no-run`, a filter) |

The sibling's rows: without `BSV_SCRIPT_SEED` the 75 `lean-seed-*` files are a kept family,
counted and not compared (rule 4: no test depends on a path outside the tree being present or
absent; the default under `$HOME` is the program's alone, never the test's). With the variable
naming the sibling's file at the pin the test regenerates and compares them (5 of 5,
10,000 rows converted).

## What needs a decision

1. The eight certificate seeds. The gate cannot be green on the base's corpus: the generator
   writes them. Built: they are checked in (seeds added, none moved). The other way is to stop
   the generator writing them; say so and the lane's follow-up removes the eight lines and files.
2. `COUNTS` and `KEPT` are pinned figures. A night's merge or a hand seed now needs its row and
   count raised in the same commit (`docs/NIGHTLY.md` says so). The looser way (families by
   name, no count) lets a merged entry in unseen; kept strict.
3. The empty seeds stay (the issue: dropping them is a separate choice). They are seven, in six
   targets, not one; two are #423's kept seeds, which the generator does not write.
