## The build once per binary

`tests/support/identity_evidence_736.rs`'s `build` keeps each shape it builds
in a `OnceLock<Mutex<HashMap<(name, size), Built>>>`, the lock held through
the build, so two tests asking for one shape at once build it once. The door
file built the 2049-input BEEF six times (the inputs test and the five
doors), the unit module twice; now once each. No assertion and no vector
changed.

Measured in the scratch copy, debug, the Studio at a load near 4:

| `tests/identity_evidence_736.rs` | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| default threads, wall | 15.52 s | 13.52 s |
| `--test-threads=1` | 24.46 s | 23.78 s |
| `query_refuses_a_limit_in_the_reference_words_736` alone | 2.90 s | 2.80 s |

The saving is about 0.7 s of work, not most of 15 s: the build is cheap. A
`sample` of the inputs test shows the time in `Beef::from_binary` (the
stream decoder and `collect`), `Beef::add_input_proof` and
`Transaction::clone_without_sources`: the parse and linking of the large
BEEF at each door, in both parsers. The unit module's whole run is under a
second. Cutting the door file's time is a change to those paths or to how
many doors parse the shape, outside a tests-only lane; stated, not done.
