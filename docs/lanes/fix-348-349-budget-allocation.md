## The finding

Under `memory_limit: Some(1_048_576)` the interpreter made one allocation of about 3.98 MB and held about 4.8 MB live: an allocation sized by the input is made before the budget is charged for it, or is charged less than it costs, or is outside the budget's count (a chunk's clone, a number's bytes, a split's halves, a cat's result, `OP_NUM2BIN`'s zero fill, the parser's push buffer, the stack's growth). The contract says the refusal comes before the allocation it would need.
