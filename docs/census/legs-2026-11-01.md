## Method

On the owner's word of 2026-11-01 14:1xZ through the keeper, the remaining interpreter and BEEF shards of the census legs ran on a quiet host from the cells; on the owner's 14:3xZ word and the keeper's 14:4xZ reading, the oracle is widened with the sibling's conformance corpus first (`BSV_CONFORMANCE_DIR` at ts-stack `edf6e03dad39bd7bc4fc6e73e64de3a2b6f805c9`, as CI's vectors job), the narrow 10-31 figures kept as the before. The method is the legs' own: `cargo-mutants` 27.1.0 under rustc 1.99.0 with `--jobs 2`, `--build-timeout 300` and the test timeout cargo-mutants sets from the unmutated baseline, the command composed by the legs' `compose.py` from the tree's `.github/scripts/nightly_mutants.py` at the base, sharded round-robin (`--no-shuffle`). The thirty cells ran on the box (18 cores, 36 GB) in two sequential streams side by side, A and B (`shards.sh`, `stream-a.log`, `stream-b.log`); the 10-31 cells of interpreter shard 0, BEEF shard 0 and Merkle 0 of 1 were re-run widened so each surface has one oracle. The load column is the mean and the max of `load.log`'s one-minute load over the cell's window; `load.log` samples from 15:35:17Z to 16:35:20Z, so a cell before it is `unsampled` (the captain's checks: about 20 at 14:38Z, about 20 to 23 at 15:28Z, on 18 cores), and a cell whose window runs past either end is marked partial. By the keeper's 15:3xZ rule a timed-out mutant is neither caught nor missed but unknown: each row reports caught of tested with the timeouts counted apart; caught/tested counts unviable mutants in the denominator, as cargo-mutants' `N mutants tested` does, and caught/viable is the nightly's score (`docs/NIGHTLY.md`: caught / (caught + missed + timeout)). The automatic timeout and the load are in every row because the host's load inflates the baseline and so the timeout.
